# Agency Studio

A reusable team for an AI creative agency managing social media brands. The human agency owner makes final content decisions; clients do not approve in the app.

## Team

- **Account Manager** — reports to the installing agency leadership.
- **Creative Strategist** — reports to account-manager.
- **Content Strategist** — reports to account-manager.
- **SEO Specialist** — reports to account-manager.
- **Marketing Strategist** — reports to account-manager.
- **Copywriter** — reports to account-manager.
- **Creative Designer** — reports to account-manager.
- **Performance Analyst** — reports to account-manager.
- **Publishing Coordinator** — reports to account-manager.

## Workflow

Owner brief → marketing/SEO research and creative idea shortlist → owner selects ideas → content plan → copy/assets → owner reviews the exact final revision → qualified publication capability or a clearly labeled manual handoff → analysis.

## Getting started

Choose Agency Studio in the existing team catalog, review its preview, select supported runtimes/accounts, and install into the intended agency. A local package can also be previewed with `paperclipai company import --from packages/teams-catalog/catalog/optional/content/agency-studio`. Check the import plan before applying it to existing data. Assign a brand-scoped task to Account Manager or Creative Strategist to begin; no recurring jobs or provider credentials are seeded.

The package does not add server-enforced content approvals, publishing integrations or brand access controls by itself. Use the product capabilities available in the installed instance.
