# Agency Studio

The company represents the agency; the human owner directs it. Clients and brands belong to that agency. An account manager coordinates eight specialists. Creative Strategist presents content ideas and insights to the owner before selected concepts become production briefs. SEO and Marketing provide evidence and campaign direction; copywriter and designer produce materials; analyst reports results; publisher prepares publication under server-enforced authority.

Only the human owner approves final content. Client approval is not required. Selecting an idea does not approve its future content revision or authorize publication. Agent instructions do not create new platform permissions. This package supplies reusable roles, not a publishing integration or automatic workflow engine. Installation uses the normal team preview, agent configuration, and governance gates; it does not auto-install into every company.
