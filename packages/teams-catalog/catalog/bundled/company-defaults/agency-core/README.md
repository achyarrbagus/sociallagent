# Agency Core

The default team for a new creative agency company. The Agency Director leads an account manager, a strategy group and a production group; the human agency owner makes final content decisions.

## Team

- **Agency Director** - top of the org; reports to the owner.
- **Account Manager** - reports to agency-director.
  - **Publishing Coordinator** - reports to account-manager.
  - **Performance Analyst** - reports to account-manager.
- **Creative Strategist** - reports to agency-director.
  - **Content Strategist**, **SEO Specialist**, **Marketing Strategist** - report to creative-strategist.
- **Creative Director** - reports to agency-director.
  - **Copywriter**, **Scriptwriter**, **Creative Designer** - report to creative-director.

## Workflow

Owner brief, research and idea shortlist, owner selects ideas, content plan, copy/scripts/assets reviewed by the Creative Director, owner reviews the exact final revision, qualified publication capability or a manual handoff, analysis.

Only the Agency Director has a recurring task (`director-review`); specialists run on assignment. The package does not add publishing integrations or brand access controls by itself.
